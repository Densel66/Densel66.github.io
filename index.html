About me

A journey of learning and growth

Densel Juma is a Kenyan aspiring entrepreneur with interests in forex markets, business development, and personal growth. He values discipline, patience, consistency, and learning from experience.

This website is a home for his professional journey, projects, and interests. It will grow over time as new milestones and achievements are added.

Forex trading

Process before promises

Densel studies price action, market structure, and trading concepts such as Smart Money Concepts (SMC) and Candle Range Theory (CRT). His learning interests include USDJPY and BTCUSD, with an emphasis on risk awareness and disciplined practice.

Risk awareness

Respecting risk and understanding that markets can move unpredictably.

Consistency

Continuous learning
